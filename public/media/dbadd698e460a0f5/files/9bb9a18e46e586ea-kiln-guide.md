# Five routes into agentic AI and what each credential earns

Researched 2026-11-18.

## Strengths and best use

The five options cover structured delegation, agent design, hands-on implementation, and a vendor exam. Pick the route that advances your next project or gives you the specific credential you need.

## Current checks

- OpenAI lists Agents and Workflows at 75-90 minutes and Design and Build Agentic Systems at 110 minutes. Academy enrollment is free with a ChatGPT account. A course badge requires completion and an assessment score of at least 80%. [OpenAI Academy course guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001270-openai-academy-courses)
- Oracle University lists the Agentic AI Foundations Associate exam as free, with 40 questions, 60 minutes, and a 65% passing score. Use MyLearn to confirm registration terms and locate the practice exam. [Oracle announcement](https://blogs.oracle.com/oracleuniversity/oracle-agentic-ai-foundations-training-certification-now-available) - [Learning path](https://mylearn.oracle.com/ou/learning-path/become-an-oracle-agentic-ai-foundations-associate-2026/163239)
- Hugging Face offers a Unit 1 fundamentals certificate. Full completion requires Unit 1, a use-case assignment, and the final challenge. IBM SkillsBuild lists an 80% assessment threshold and requires learners to be registered with a participating organization for this badge. [Hugging Face course](https://huggingface.co/learn/agents-course/en/unit0/introduction) - [IBM badge criteria](https://www.credly.com/org/ibm-skillsbuild/badge/make-agentic-ai-work-for-you)

## Project idea

Build a small agent that can look up one local fixture and decline requests outside its tool scope. Include a successful run, one tool failure, and one restricted request. Use the worksheet to pair the artifact with course time, eligibility, and the exact credential name. Add separate time for exam preparation and scheduling.
