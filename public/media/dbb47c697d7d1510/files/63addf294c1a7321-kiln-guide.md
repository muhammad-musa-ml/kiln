Researched 2026-11-07.

## Strengths and best use
AI evaluation platforms let people apply domain knowledge to response ranking, rubrics, data review, and specialized tasks. The useful opportunity is matching demonstrable expertise to an actual project. This reel identifies CrowdGen, Mindrift, micro1, and Toloka as discovery leads. ## Compare an application carefully
Open a role through the official site and record its URL, eligibility, screening process, stated rate, task availability, payment conditions, and date checked. Estimate assessment, training, waiting, and rework time alongside paid task time. Preserve uncertainty when details are absent. Follow the task's instructions about tools and AI assistance.

Use a sample evaluation to demonstrate reasoning. Explain a ranking, identify factual errors, and build a rubric with observable criteria. Review the complete instructions before starting any qualification exercise.
