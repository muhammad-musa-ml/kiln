"Remote" at company level does not establish that every job accepts every location, time zone or experience level. The reel's broad compensation and worldwide-access claims should be checked against each live posting. Circle here is not the stablecoin company with a similar name.

## Screen an actual role
1. Open the company's official careers page and follow its own posting link. Record the URL and the date checked, since openings change.
2. Read the exact title and scope. Distinguish product engineering, infrastructure, security and customer-facing roles rather than applying to the company name alone.
3. Copy the posting's stated location and time-zone conditions. Leave missing information as unknown; do not infer permission from "remote" in a marketing page.
4. Record experience requirements and compensation only if stated for that role. Do not convert a headline's salary into a universal company band.
5. Match requirements to actual evidence: a relevant project, a clear explanation of decisions and a demonstrable contribution. Apply through the official route after checking the instructions.

## Preparation by product area
For Supabase and Railway, a small deployed application with logs, database changes and an operational explanation can demonstrate relevant work. For OpenZeppelin, inspect the role's security and blockchain requirements before choosing a practice task. For Circle, focus on community-product workflows; for RevenueCat, understand subscription lifecycle concepts. These are independent preparation suggestions, not employer interview promises or instructions to use their production systems.

```text
location, time-zone, experience and compensation conditions without
guessing. Separate supported matches, gaps and questions for a recruiter.
Suggest one small portfolio improvement grounded in real requirements.
Do not invent eligibility, submit an application or contact anyone.
```
