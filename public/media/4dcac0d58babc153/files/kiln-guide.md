## Who the program is for

The paid, full-time research placement is intended for established technical leaders making a transition into AI research. Anthropic names staff engineers, technical founders, and quantitative professors as examples. The postings accept a bachelor's degree or equivalent experience. A PhD, publications, and prior ML experience are optional. Demonstrated ownership of ambitious technical work matters more than the absence of those requirements. [Program announcement](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/anthropic-residency/) - [Research Resident posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5438311008)

The listed durations are six weeks, three months, and six months. The $350,000-$500,000 figure is annualized and prorated. Six weeks at that rate is roughly $40,000-$58,000 before taxes using a 52-week approximation. The employer prefers San Francisco and describes remote participation for some placements. The postings discuss sponsorship for placements of at least three months and encourage eligible remote arrangements for six-week placements. Ask the employer to confirm arrangements for a particular location and duration. [US posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5438311008) - [UK posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5439169008)

## Prepare a research pitch

1. Choose one area from the announcement, such as model oversight, interpretability, or AI security. Read a relevant project from that team and write one question whose answer would change a research decision.
2. Collect two examples of technical leadership. State the problem, what you decided, how you measured results, and what you learned when an assumption failed.
3. Draft a one-page experiment with a baseline, an evaluation protocol, a short milestone, and a stop condition. Scope the first result to your requested placement length.
4. Compare your evidence with both the Residency and the separately posted Fellows Program. Check the Fellows listing directly for its own eligibility and timing. [Fellows posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5183044008)

## Practice project

Build a small oversight evaluation with mocked tools and synthetic examples. Compare a baseline prompt with an explicit approval rule. Record disagreements by category and publish a reproducible methods note using sanitized examples.
