# Five launch communities and a first-use experiment

Researched 2026-11-12.

## Strengths and best use

The reel gives founders several places to find feedback: Reddit SideProject, Show HN, Product Hunt, Indie Hackers and Uneed. Pick the community where people can evaluate a task your product already supports.

## Current checks

Show HN asks for something people can try and recommends avoiding signup barriers. Product Hunt publishes its own launch guide. Uneed documents a free waiting line with scheduling and publication conditions. Check the subreddit's rules and Indie Hackers' current posting options before sharing. The communities provide distribution opportunities. The reel's fictional app provides no measured acquisition result.

[Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) - [Product Hunt Launch Guide](https://help.producthunt.com/en/collections/19793093-launch-guide) - [Reddit SideProject rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/about/rules) - [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/) - [Uneed launch rules](https://www.uneed.best/how-it-works)

## Project idea

Give each channel a distinct campaign link and hold the landing experience steady. Count eligible visits, first completed actions, useful feedback and seven-day return. Write a short account of what people tried and where they got stuck before committing more time to promotion.

## Reusable prompt

```text
Plan one measured launch for a working demo. Choose a channel using its current rules, define the first useful action, and specify tracking for visits, activation, feedback and repeat use.
```
