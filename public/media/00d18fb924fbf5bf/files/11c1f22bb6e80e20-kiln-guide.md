# Clipping campaigns: rights, submissions and settled earnings

Researched 2026-11-07.

## Strengths and best use
The reel directs attention to the constraints that decide whether clipping work gets paid. Campaign fit, minimum qualifying views, a remaining budget, and submission timing belong in the production checklist. Content Rewards documents CPM, per-post, and retainer models, so the right comparison begins with the actual brief. [Official help](https://help.contentrewards.com/en/articles/15319790-how-content-rewards-works)

## Current checks
The current help page says creators submit links within 30 minutes. CPM payments have campaign minima and caps, and approval starts a further earning period and hold. Creator fees have exceptions for some larger per-post and retainer campaigns. Save the current brief and fee terms before calculating returns. The reel's universal five-post shadow-ban threshold and blanket rejection of AI edits lack supporting evidence in the inspected sources. [Campaign mechanics](https://help.contentrewards.com/en/articles/15319790-how-content-rewards-works) - [Whop overview](https://whop.com/blog/whop-content-rewards/)

## Screen one campaign
Record permitted source material, allowed edits, eligible accounts, qualifying audience, minimum views, rate, cap, budget, deadline, fee, and review criteria. Confirm that you have permission for every source and music track. Start with one compliant clip and track approval before increasing output. Keep pending and settled earnings in separate columns.

## Reusable prompt
```text
Extract the payment and content conditions from this campaign brief. Preserve unknown values. Calculate qualifying revenue, caps, fees, and time spent using supplied inputs. Build a submission checklist with the deadline and source permissions. Flag ambiguity for the campaign owner.
```
