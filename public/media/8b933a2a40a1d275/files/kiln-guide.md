# SideShift creator work: briefs, usage rights and effective pay

Researched 2026-11-07.

## Strengths and best use
SideShift provides a concrete route to creator briefs, applications, deliverable review, and payment management. It can help creators package a portfolio around a brand's specific need. Its official creator page describes the process and links the application surface. [Creator page](https://sideshift.app/creators)

## Check the economics
The reel divides a claimed $1,200 payment by three filming hours to get $400 per hour. Its screen shows a monthly amount. Effective hourly pay requires all time used for applications, setup, scripting, retakes, editing, posting, revisions, and administration. Portfolio selection and a campaign award also precede paid production. The named brand examples and earnings remain creator or platform reports.

## Prepare a testable offer
1. Choose a product you understand and make two original sample videos with different openings.
2. Save the actual campaign brief, deliverable count, revision allowance, publishing obligations, usage rights, deadlines, approval conditions, and payment terms.
3. Estimate every production step and keep the accepted scope visible when reviewing revisions.
4. Log accepted deliverables, settled revenue, expenses, and all work time. Compare at least a complete campaign cycle before choosing a repeatable workload.

## Reusable prompt
```text
Read this creator brief and extract deliverables, deadlines, revisions, usage rights, approval, and payment conditions. Build a realistic time estimate including unpaid preparation. Draft a portfolio sample plan grounded in product features I can demonstrate, and flag missing contract details.
```

## Project idea
